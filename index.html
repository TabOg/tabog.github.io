Tabitha Ogilvie

Hi! I'm a Research Associate (postdoc) at King's College London, working with Martin Albrecht in the Cryptography Group. I completed my PhD as part of the Information Security Group at Royal Holloway, University of London, supervised by Elizabeth Quaglia.

I work on lattice based cryptography, with a particular focus on the security of homomorphic encryption. I am also interested in cryptanalysis and the concrete hardness of lattice problems, and I contribute to the Lattice Estimator. During my PhD I interned at Intel and Intel Labs, working on deploying homomorphic encryption, as well as at Apple, where my former team have since published a blog post and given a talk at RWC 2025 about their work.

Always happy to chat about lattices and homomorphic encryption, so feel free to get in touch!
